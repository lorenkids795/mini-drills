# Mini Drill — visual asset requirements

For Mini Drill interface or visual asset work, including Vocab Lab Phase 1 and all later phases, read and follow `docs/CODEX-VISUAL-ASSETS-NO-AI.md` before implementation. This policy was explicitly adopted by the project owner on 2026-10-28.

- Do not generate or ship AI images/illustrations. Earlier AI mockups are concept references only: do not embed, copy, trace or recreate their recognizable artwork.
- Prefer HTML/CSS and original hand-authored SVG/CSS geometry. Reuse existing assets only when their non-AI provenance and usage rights are suitable.
- Pinterest is for research and high-level inspiration only; it does not grant an asset license.
- Before integrating third-party production artwork, verify its original source and commercial/public-web rights, record creator/license/attribution in `docs/VOCAB-LAB-ASSET-REGISTER.md`, and obtain the project owner's approval. This approval requirement comes directly from the adopted visual asset policy.
- Without eligible artwork, use abstract CSS/SVG placeholders. Future puzzle boards/pieces use HTML/CSS/SVG, award only still-missing pieces, and keep rewards separate from vocabulary mastery.
- Maintain responsive/mobile behavior, accessibility, performance and reduced-motion support.

For Vocab Lab tasks, continue on `feature/vocab-lab` and edit only the repository root source tree. Do not edit the divergent nested `mini-drills/` copy, `main`, Cloudflare configuration/deployment or Writing Module without an explicit new user instruction. This policy does not authorize expanding Phase 1 into future missions or puzzles.
